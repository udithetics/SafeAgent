How SAFE.AGENT Works

A complete, autonomous lifecycle. From watching your data to fixing your AI behind the scenes without humans lifting a finger.

1. The Watcher

SafeAgent attaches to your AI and quietly observes. It logs every single prediction your model makes to understand what a "normal" day looks like.

2. Reality Shifts

The real world is messy. Trends change, users act differently, and data evolves. Suddenly, the old data your AI learned from doesn't match reality anymore.

3. Drift Detected

Our Z-Score math kicks in. SafeAgent instantly spots that the AI is getting confused. The risk score spikes on your dashboard, sounding the alarm.

4. Quarantine

Before the AI can make costly mistakes for your business, the Agent intervenes. It isolates the toxic, confusing data so it can't cause more damage.

5. The Forge

SafeAgent acts as an automated mechanic. It takes the fresh, clean data from the real world and rapidly trains a brand new, smarter version of your AI.

6. Seamless Swap

The old model is retired. The new, highly accurate model is hot-swapped into place. Your system is fully healed, and your users never even notice a hiccup.

System Protocols & Terms

Please review our operational guidelines before initializing a SafeAgent node.

1. Acceptance of Protocols

By accessing the SafeAgent network, logging into the dashboard, or utilizing our API endpoints, you agree to be bound by these System Protocols. If you do not agree to all terms and conditions, you must immediately disconnect your models from our monitoring nodes.

2. Data Privacy & Ingestion

SafeAgent only ingests prediction inputs and confidence scores. We do not store personally identifiable information (PII) from your end-users. All telemetry data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and is automatically purged during the "Self-Healing" (retraining) phase.

3. Autonomous Retraining Liability

While the "Auto-Repair" function is designed to mitigate model drift, the Operator assumes all responsibility for the final business logic executed by the newly trained model. SafeAgent provides anomaly detection; human oversight is recommended for critical infrastructure.

4. API Quota & Usage

Accounts found attempting to bypass rate limits or reverse-engineer the Z-Score drift detection algorithm will face immediate node termination. Ensure your `.env` keys remain secure.

5. Modifications to the Grid

We reserve the right to modify or replace these Terms at any time. Major protocol updates will be transmitted to the System Email on file 30 days prior to execution.
