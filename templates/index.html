How SAFE.AGENT Works

A complete, autonomous lifecycle. From watching your data to fixing your AI behind the scenes without humans lifting a finger.

1. The Watcher

SafeAgent attaches to your AI and quietly observes. It logs every single prediction your model makes to understand what a "normal" day looks like.

2. Reality Shifts

The real world is messy. Trends change, users act differently, and data evolves. Suddenly, the old data your AI learned from doesn't match reality anymore.

3. Drift Detected

Our Z-Score math kicks in. SafeAgent instantly spots that the AI is getting confused. The risk score spikes on your dashboard, sounding the alarm.

4. Quarantine

Before the AI can make costly mistakes for your business, the Agent intervenes. It isolates the toxic, confusing data so it can't cause more damage.

5. The Forge

SafeAgent acts as an automated mechanic. It takes the fresh, clean data from the real world and rapidly trains a brand new, smarter version of your AI.

6. Seamless Swap

The old model is retired. The new, highly accurate model is hot-swapped into place. Your system is fully healed, and your users never even notice a hiccup.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At SafeAgent, we build infrastructure to monitor and heal machine learning models. Because our systems interface directly with your data pipelines, privacy and data minimization are core to our architecture.

1. Telemetry & Data Ingestion

SafeAgent acts as a watcher node. To calculate Z-Scores and detect model drift, we ingest:

  • Prediction inputs and outputs generated by your connected models.
  • Confidence scores and metadata regarding model performance.
  • System health metrics (latency, resource utilization).

2. Prohibition of PII

We do not want, nor do we intentionally store, your end-users' personal data. It is the Operator's responsibility to anonymize or hash PII before data is routed through the SafeAgent monitoring protocol.

3. Ephemeral Storage

Data flagged during a "drift event" is temporarily quarantined solely for the purpose of automated retraining. Once a new model is successfully hot-swapped, the underlying quarantined data is permanently purged.

System Protocols (Terms)

Last Updated: April 2026

By accessing the SafeAgent network, logging into the dashboard, or utilizing our Model Context Protocol API endpoints, you agree to be bound by these System Protocols.

1. Operator Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your SafeAgent account, API keys, and deployment credentials. You must not use our automated systems to monitor malware, unauthorized scrapers, or illegal applications.

2. Autonomous Retraining Liability

SafeAgent features an "Auto-Repair" function designed to mitigate model drift. While our algorithms are heavily tested, the Operator assumes all responsibility for the final business logic executed by the newly trained model.

3. API Quotas & Fair Use
